# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want every project in this organization to be a welcoming and respectful space for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions, issues, and pull requests.
- Welcome newcomers and help them get oriented.
- Give constructive feedback — focus on the code and ideas, not the person.
- Accept disagreement gracefully; not every suggestion will be adopted, and that's okay.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory jokes, language, or content.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or project work.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please open a private report by contacting the maintainers directly through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed and handled as discreetly as possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues/PRs, or restrict participation for anyone who violates this Code of Conduct.

By participating in this project, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct.
